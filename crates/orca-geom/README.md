# orca-geom

Clipper 6.4.2, ClipperUtils and libslic3r's geometry: points, polygons, ExPolygons, boxes, hulls.

The geometry everything after the model's reading stands on: Clipper 6.4.2 as OrcaSlicer vendors it (`deps_src/clipper`, compiled with `Slic3r::Point` as its `IntPoint`), with `Int128` as `i128`; libslic3r's `ClipperUtils`; and libslic3r's geometry: `Point`, `Polygon`, `Polyline`, `Line`, `MultiPoint` (Douglas-Peucker; Visvalingam is left out, as nothing calls it and its code is undefined behaviour), `ExPolygon` (without the medial axis), `BoundingBox`, the predicates, angles, transform builders and convex hulls of `Geometry.cpp` and `Geometry/ConvexHull.cpp`, and `scale_` and `unscale` with the command line's `SCALING_FACTOR` of 1e-6. `coord_t` is `i64`. Phase O3 (orca-o3-geom-1, 2026-11-13) adds ClipperLib_Z, `Circle`, `ArcSegment` and `ArcFitter`, `Polyline`'s `fitting_result`, `ThickPolyline` and `ThickLine`, and the 2D helpers the extrusion paths use; orca-o3-geom-2 (2026-11-13) adds the 3D geometry of non-planar printing, which an `ExtrusionPath`'s `Polyline3` holds: `Point3`, `Line3`, `BoundingBox3`, `MultiPoint3` with its Douglas-Peucker, `Polyline3` with its arc fitting, the offset of a `Polyline3` and the `Points3` forms of `Circle`'s and `ArcSegment`'s fits. Not here yet: `Geometry::Transformation` and `TransformationSVD`, `Geometry::arrange`, and the ClipperUtils overloads on `Surface`s and the chained traversals that need ShortestPath (`parity/HAZARDS.md`, orca-geom, lists them).

## Modules

- `clipper`: Clipper 6.4.2 whole (`deps_src/clipper/clipper.cpp`). `engine.rs` is `ClipperBase` and `Clipper`: edges, local minima, the scanbeam, the active and sorted edge lists, intersections, horizontals, joins, and the output records built into `Paths` or a `PolyTree`. `offset.rs` is `ClipperOffset`; `polytree.rs` is `PolyNode` and `PolyTree`, the nodes in one vector linked by index; `mod.rs` holds the types and the free functions (`Area`, `PointInPolygon`, `SimplifyPolygon(s)`, `CleanPolygon(s)`, `Minkowski*`, the `PolyTree` conversions). The C++ pointers are indices into arenas, allocated in the C++ order. Sorts and the scanbeam's `std::priority_queue` go through `orca-stl` (MSVC's introsort and heap, so ties fall the same way), `acos`, `sin`, `cos`, `atan2` and the float-to-integer casts through `orca-crt`. An exception Clipper throws to its caller (`RangeTest`, an open clip path, `Execute` to `Paths` after an open path) is a panic whose payload is a `ClipperException`, which `catch_unwind` and `downcast` catch as `catch (clipperException&)` does; the two it catches itself (`UpdateEdgeIntoAEL`, `DoMaxima`) are a `Result` inside. The `ClipperLib_Z` sites are marked `ClipperLib_Z` in `engine.rs`; the Z build itself is `clipper_z`. Clipper2 is not here.
- `clipper_z`: ClipperLib_Z, the engine's second build of `clipper.cpp` (`clipper_z.cpp` with `CLIPPERLIB_USE_XYZ`), which `PerimeterGenerator`'s `clip_extrusion` and `LineSplit` use. `engine.rs` and `polytree.rs` repeat the XY `clipper` files line for line on an `IntPoint` of x, y and z, with `ZFillFunction`, `SetZ` at its four call sites and the z of `IntersectPoint`, `ReverseHorizontal` and the top of the scanbeam; where the C++ hands `SetZ` an edge's own `Curr` or `Top`, the z is written back there. Inside the engine every point comparison is of x and y only (`pt_eq`), as clipper.cpp's own `operator==` (lines 108-110) overrides Eigen's in that build, except in `AddPaths` of more than one path: a template of clipper.hpp, compiled in its caller's file where that operator is not seen, it trims a closing point and repeated end points by Eigen's `==` of x, y and z. The public `IntPoint`'s `==` compares x, y and z, as Eigen's does for the engine's callers. `mod.rs` holds the types (the enums, `IntRect` and `ClipperException` are the XY module's), `Area` and `PolyTreeToPaths`. Left out, as no engine code reaches them in `ClipperLib_Z`: `ClipperOffset`, `Orientation`, `PointInPolygon`, `SimplifyPolygon(s)`, `CleanPolygon(s)`, the Minkowski functions, `Closed`/`OpenPathsFromPolyTree` and `ReversePath(s)`.
- `circle`: `Circle.hpp` and `Circle.cpp`: `Circle` (the circle of three points and of a run of points, the polar angle, the deviation tests, the foot on a segment, the tangent) and `ArcSegment` (the constructor, `reverse`, `clip_start`, `clip_end`, `split_at`, `is_point_inside`, `try_create_arc`, `are_points_within_slice`, `ray_intersects_segment` and the `float` `calc_arc_*`). C++ out-parameters written only on success are `Option`s; `try_create_circle` of a run keeps its `&mut Circle`, which a failed fit can still change. `are_points_within_slice` tests only the last two points, as the C++ loop starts at `point_count - 2`. The `Points3` forms (geom-2: `try_create_circle_points3`, `try_create_arc_points3`, `are_points_within_slice_points3`) work on the points' x and y and give what the 2D forms give for them.
- `arc_fitter`: `ArcFitter.hpp` and `ArcFitter.cpp`: `EMovePathType`, `PathFittingData`, `do_arc_fitting` and `do_arc_fitting_and_simplify`, with `size_t` indices that wrap as the C++'s do.
- `int128`: `Int128.hpp`'s filtered predicates. MSVC compiles its `_mul128` branch, whose exact products are `i128`; the estimate adds `1 << 31`, which MSVC makes `INT_MIN`, so it subtracts half a word.
- `point`: `coord_t` (`i64`), `Point` (`Eigen::Matrix<coord_t, 2, 1, DontAlign>`), `scale_`, `unscale` and the point helpers of `Point.hpp`. Its arithmetic wraps, as MSVC's does. `cwiseMin` and `cwiseMax` are Eigen's SSE2 `pmin` and `pmax` of a `Packet2l`, whose 64-bit compare takes the low 32-bit halves signed (`orca_eigen::pcmp_lt_i64`): past 2^31 they can pick the wrong coordinate, and a `BoundingBox` then misses a point, as the engine's does.
- `line`: `Line` and `line_alg`'s distances, intersections and angles.
- `multi_point`: `MultiPoint` as functions of a point slice (the C++ class's virtual `lines()` becomes an argument), with `_douglas_peucker` and `get_extents_rotated`.
- `polygon`, `polyline`, `expolygon`: `Polygon`, `Polyline` and `ExPolygon` with their free helpers (`remove_sticks`, `remove_collinear`, `polygons_simplify`, `make_circle`, `foot_pt`, `keep_largest_contour_only`, the `to_*` conversions and `get_extents*`). C++ out-parameters that report success become `Option`s (`Polyline::split_at_length`). `Polyline` carries `fitting_result`, and every method keeps it in step as `Polyline.hpp` and `Polyline.cpp` do (appends, `reverse`, `clip_end`, the splits, `simplify_by_fitting_arc`; `simplify` and `clear` drop it); `==` compares the points alone. `append(Polyline&&)` is `append_polyline_moved(&mut src)`, which leaves `src` empty. `remove_degenerate` swaps only the points into place, as the C++ does, so a kept polyline takes the fit of its new slot. `ThickPolyline` (a `Polyline` through `Deref`, with two widths per segment) and `ThickLine` are in `polyline.rs`, as no other piece carries them.
- `bounding_box`: `BoundingBox`, `BoundingBoxf` and `BoundingBoxf3` with `transformed`.
- `point3`: `Point3` (a `Vec3crd`: three `i64`s, wrapping arithmetic), `Points3`, `Line3`, the 3D `line_alg` distance, `BoundingBox3`, `lerp` and the `Points`/`Points3` conversions. Most methods work on x and y and keep z (`rotate`, `ccw`, `nearest_point`, `both_comp`, `any_comp`); the member `operator*` rounds (`mul_round`), the free one and `*=` truncate (`mul_trunc`, `scale_assign`). `projection_onto(Line3)` projects in x and y and takes z along the line by the 2D distance over the 3D length; `projection_onto(MultiPoint3)` always throws in C++, an `Err` of its text here. `BoundingBox3` takes x and y by Eigen's `Packet2l` `pmin` and `pmax` (wrong past 2^31) and z alone, through orca-eigen's `cwise_min` and `cwise_max`. Left out: `BoundingBox3(const std::vector<Vec3crd>&)`, which nothing in domain A builds.
- `multi_point3`: `MultiPoint3`'s members as functions of a Point3 slice (`translate`, the rotations, `find_point` of a `Point` by x and y or of a `Point3` by all three, exact or within an epsilon, `remove_duplicate_points`, `bounding_box`, the length of the lines) and the 3D `_douglas_peucker`.
- `polyline3`: `Polyline3` with its `fitting_result`, kept in step as `Polyline`'s is (reverse, `clip_end`, `split_at_index`, `split_at` of a `Point` or a `Point3`, `split_at_length`, the appends, `simplify`, `simplify_by_fitting_arc`), `to_polyline` of `Points` and of `Points3`, and `foot_pt` of a `Points3`. The splits take their halves as `&mut` out-parameters, as the C++ does, because `clear()` is `MultiPoint3::clear()`, which keeps the fit: a half that held a fit can carry it into an append. `simplify_by_fitting_arc` fits x and y and gives every point z 0; a clipped arc's new end in a split has z 0, in `clip_end` it keeps the last point's z. `Polyline3(const Polyline&, z)` takes no fit.
- `clipper_utils`: `ClipperUtils.cpp`: the offsets (`offset`, `offset_ex`, `offset2_ex`, `closing`, `opening`, `expand`, `shrink`), the boolean operations on polygons, ExPolygons, polylines and lines, `union_safety_offset`, `simplify_polygons`, `top_level_islands`, `fix_after_*_offset`, the variable-width offsets (`mittered_offset_path_scaled`, `variable_offset_*`), the bounding box clip of `ClipperUtils::clip_clipper_polygons_with_subject_bbox`, and `offset_polyline3`, the offset of a `Polyline3`'s x and y (geom-2). The C++ overloads are functions named for their argument types (`diff_ex_expolygons`, `intersection_pl_polygons`); a path provider (`PathsProvider`, `ExPolygonsProvider`) is a list of point slices, `PathsRef`, an ExPolygon giving its contour, then its holes.
- `geometry`: `Geometry.hpp` and `Geometry.cpp`: `orient`, the segment and ray intersections, `foot_pt`, the Liang-Barsky clip, `directions_parallel`, the angle folds, `assemble_transform`, `rotation_transform`, `scale_transform`, `extract_euler_angles`, `rotation_xyz_diff` and `rotation_diff_z` (Eigen's quaternion products, left to right), `is_point_inside_polygon_corner`, and `ray_circle_intersections_r2_lv2_c` of `Geometry/Circle.hpp`. Geometry.cpp's `dist` and `project_point_to_segment` are templates nothing instantiates and are not carried.
- `convex_hull`: `Geometry/ConvexHull.cpp`: the convex hull of points and the one of `Vec3d`s in x and y (`convex_hull_3d`), `convex_polygons_intersect` (rotating calipers) and the top and bottom chains of a convex polygon with `inside_convex_polygon`.

Sorts, heaps, selections and float min and max go through `orca-stl`, maths and float-to-integer casts through `orca-crt`, the vectors and transforms through `orca-eigen`, with `f32` where the C++ has `float` (the ClipperUtils deltas) and the C++ order of evaluation.

## Probe gate

`tests/probes.rs` replays the records of `geom.sop1` but the Eigen ones, which orca-eigen replays (`parity/SOP1.md`, "geom.sop1"): CCLP (Clipper runs), COFF (offsets), CARE (`Area`, `Orientation`, `PointInPolygon`), CCLN (`CleanPolygon`), CSIM (`SimplifyPolygon(s)`), CMNK (Minkowski), CINT (`Int128`), GPNT (`Point`, `Line` and the `Geometry` predicates), GPLY (`Polygon`, `Polyline`, `MultiPoint`, `BoundingBox`, the 2D hull), GCLU (ClipperUtils and `ExPolygon`) and GXFM (the transform builders, `BoundingBoxf3::transformed`, the hull of `Vec3d`s, `make_circle`, `convex_polygons_intersect`, the convex chains). Every result is compared bit for bit, a `double` by its bits. `bun run engine parity probe --only geom` writes the file from the C++ engine's own build (`parity/oracle/probe/geom/clipper.cpp` and `geometry.cpp`): the committed subset in `parity/probes/` runs by default, the full set with `SKETCH_ORCA_PROBES_DEEP=<work>/parity/probes`. A record kind nothing replays fails the test.

`tests/paths_sop1/mod.rs` (a module of `tests/probes.rs`) replays the records of `paths.sop1` whose sections in `parity/sop1/paths.md` name the piece geom-1: PSZC (ClipperLib_Z with its Z values, the callback's calls and the tree's nodes), PSRZ (`rotation_xyz_diff`, `rotation_diff_z`), PSIC (`is_point_inside_polygon_corner`), PSCV (`convex_points`, `concave_points`), PSRC (`ray_circle_intersections_r2_lv2_c`), PSCI (`Circle`), PSAS and PSAC (`ArcSegment`), PSAF (`ArcFitter`), PSPF (`Polyline`'s fitting results) and PSTP (`ThickPolyline`). `tests/paths_sop1/geom2.rs` replays those whose sections name geom-2: PSP3 (`Point3`, `Line3`, `BoundingBox3`, `MultiPoint3`'s members, `foot_pt` of a `Points3`), PSD3 (the 3D Douglas-Peucker), PSL3 (`Polyline3` with and without a fit), PSC3 (the `Points3` arc fits) and PSO3 (`offset_polyline3`). A kind of no piece fails the test; the kinds of paths-1 are replayed by orca-paths.

## Stops

Inputs the C++ reads outside its arrays for (`parity/sop1/paths.md`, "Edges") panic with a message naming the call rather than guess: `Circle::try_create_circle` of fewer than two points, `is_over_deviation` and `get_deviation_sum_squared` of none, `ArcSegment::try_create_arc` of fewer than two, `are_points_within_slice` of fewer than three, `Polygon::convex_points` and `concave_points` of fewer than two, `Polyline::split_at_index` of an empty polyline, `ThickPolyline::start_at_index` outside [0, size) on a closed polyline, `to_thick_polylines` of a polyline with no points, the `Points3` forms of `try_create_arc` of fewer than two points and of `are_points_within_slice` of fewer than three, and `Polyline3`'s `first_point` and `last_point` of an empty polyline (with them `split_at_index` and `clip_end` of one).
